# Product Context

App name: FitMeal AI

FitMeal AI is an iOS app that combines:
- AI meal planner
- AI workout planner
- Habit tracker
- Progress dashboard
- Free, Silver, and Gold plans

Target users:
- Busy professionals
- Beginners in fitness
- People who want simple meal planning
- People who want simple home workout guidance

Core value:
Help users eat better and move smarter with simple daily AI plans.

Primary UX goal:
Make the app feel premium, simple, and native to iOS.

Main screens:
- Splash
- Login (email + phone OTP, Google, Apple)
- Onboarding Goal
- Onboarding Workout (multi-select workout types, days/week, duration)
- Onboarding Meal (multi-select diets, meal timings, cook time, allergies)
- Onboarding Diet (legacy, kept for fallback)
- Home Dashboard
- AI Generating State
- Meal Plan (with ingredient breakdown modal)
- Workout
- Habits
- Progress (with period switching, weight trend, calories)
- Paywall
- ABA Payment QR
- Payment Pending
- Settings
- Settings Workout (edit saved workout preferences)
- Settings Meal (edit saved meal preferences)

Brand note:
Internal/legacy assets may still reference "VitaGlass AI". Final brand is "FitMeal AI" - all user-facing copy should use FitMeal AI.
