# Product Context

App name: FitMeal AI

FitMeal AI is an iOS app that combines:
- AI meal planner
- AI workout planner
- Habit tracker
- Progress dashboard
- Free, Silver, and Gold plans

Target users:
- Busy professionals
- Beginners in fitness
- People who want simple meal planning
- People who want simple home workout guidance

Core value:
Help users eat better and move smarter with simple daily AI plans.

Primary UX goal:
Make the app feel premium, simple, and native to iOS.

Main screens:
- Splash
- Onboarding Goal
- Onboarding Diet
- Home Dashboard
- AI Generating State
- Meal Plan
- Workout
- Habits
- Progress
- Paywall
- ABA Payment QR
- Payment Pending
- Settings
